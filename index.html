Transfers Between Children

Transfer an amount between two child accounts. The app records two matching Credit / Adjustment entries using positive amounts, so the database does not need to accept negative transaction amounts.

Debit increases the From child's balance and decreases the To child's balance. Credit decreases the From child's balance and increases the To child's balance.

Bulk Ledger Entry

Review and save multiple paper-ledger entries to your connected Supabase database. Nothing is saved until you select Save reviewed entries. The beginning balance is not changed by this tool.

Use dates as MM-DD-YYYY. Example: Charge | Regular daycare | 01-05-2026 | 01-05-2026 | 01-09-2026 | 230 | | Paper ledger. For a payment, use Payment and enter its method, such as Cash, Check, Cash App, Direct deposit, or CCAP. Amounts should be positive numbers.
