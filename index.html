Split Family Payment — Monthly or Weekly

Split one family payment between two children using their saved Regular daycare or CCAP charges for the selected period. Preview the shares before saving; no charges are changed.

Whole month counts saved Regular daycare and CCAP charges starting in that month. Date range counts complete Monday–Friday daycare weeks starting within your chosen dates. Check that both children's weekly charges are present; weeks spanning two months count by their Monday start date.

Billing Error Checker

Monthly Charge Checker — Non-CCAP Children

Compares all payments dated in the selected month with daycare charges for weeks starting that month, without assigning payments to individual weeks. Checks missing weekly charges separately. Children without charges in the selected month are skipped. Weeks are grouped by their Monday start date. Read-only: nothing is added or changed.

Transfers Between Children

Transfer an amount between two child accounts. The app records two matching Credit / Adjustment entries using positive amounts, so the database does not need to accept negative transaction amounts.

Debit increases the From child's balance and decreases the To child's balance. Credit decreases the From child's balance and increases the To child's balance.

Bulk Ledger Entry

Review and save multiple paper-ledger entries to your connected Supabase database. Nothing is saved until you select Save reviewed entries. The beginning balance is not changed by this tool.

Use dates as MM-DD-YYYY. Example: Charge | Regular daycare | 01-05-2026 | 01-05-2026 | 01-09-2026 | 230 | | Paper ledger. For a payment, use Payment and enter its method, such as Cash, Check, Cash App, Direct deposit, or CCAP. Amounts should be positive numbers.
